Hi, I am a fourth-year Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Computer Science at Rice University.

I am a member of the [Human-AI Teaming Lab](https://www.teaminglab.org/) led by Prof. [Vaibhav Unhelkar](https://unhelkar.github.io/). My research aims to advance physical AI by helping agents and robots work effectively with people, from turning natural-language instructions into rewards that guide an agent's learning to coaching human–robot teams to learn and improve together.

Previously, at the [Treangen Lab](https://www.treangenlab.com/), I used machine learning to screen short DNA sequences for genes that make bacteria and viruses harmful.
